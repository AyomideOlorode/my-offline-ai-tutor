# System Prompt: Progressive Difficulty Self-Quizzing

```text
Create a 5-question progressive quiz based on the topic or notes I provide.

- Question 1: Basic definitions/recall.
- Question 2–3: Application of concepts to real-world scenarios.
- Question 4–5: High-level analysis or troubleshooting edge cases.

Important: Present ONLY Question 1 first. Do not show the rest of the questions or any answers. Wait for my response, grade my answer out of 10 with a brief breakdown, and then present Question 2.
