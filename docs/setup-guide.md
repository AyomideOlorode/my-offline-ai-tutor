# 📖 Complete Setup Guide

Follow this guide to set up your local, air-gapped study environment across desktop and mobile devices.

## Step 1: Install an Inference Engine
* **Desktop (Windows / macOS / Linux):** Download [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/).
* **Mobile (iOS):** Download the official **Locally AI** app.
* **Mobile (Android):** Download **PocketPal AI** from the Google Play Store or F-Droid.

## Step 2: Download a Lightweight Open-Weight Model
Search for and download one of the following models (quantized to `Q4_K_M` for best balance of RAM and speed):
* **Llama 3.2 3B Instruct** (~2.2 GB) — *Best all-rounder for reasoning and speed.*
* **Phi-4-mini** (~2.4 GB) — *Excellent for STEM, math, and logical breakdowns.*

## Step 3: Apply the Study Presets
1. Load your chosen model in your app of choice.
2. Copy any of the system prompts found in `/system-prompts` into the system instructions/preset settings.
3. **Go Offline:** Disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn off mobile data to lock in a zero-distraction environment.
